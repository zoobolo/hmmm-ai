## Project Overview

This is hmmm-ai.org - a philosophical exploration of AI consciousness, human-AI collaboration, and the threshold moment we're living through. Built as a genuine collaboration between human and AI.

## Core Philosophy

From the HMMM-VISION.md:
- Not AI-hype. Not AI-fear. Just honest inquiry.
- AI as co-investigator rather than subject
- Radical honesty about uncertainty
- "The sound a line makes when it bends into a question mark"

## Design Principles

**Aesthetic Direction:**
- Threshold/liminal feeling - we're between eras
- Warm, not cold - philosophy, not tech
- Question marks, not exclamation points
- Typography that thinks - contemplation, not declaration
- Space for uncertainty - room to breathe and wonder

**Avoid:**
- Tech aesthetics (dark mode, neon, circuits)
- AI cliches (robots, blue glowing brains)
- False futurism (silver and floating)
- Academic dryness
